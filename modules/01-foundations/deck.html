The diagnostic — twelve questions

Pen and paper.
No pausing to search.

  • Write down your answer before I give minea half-formed answer still counts as a mark
  • Score one point per question you got substantially right
  • Twelve questions, in two parts of six

Your score is not a verdict

It is an address. Every question you miss points at a specific module number — and that module already has a lesson written for exactly that gap.

Most experienced React developers score between 4 and 6. That is the normal result, not the bad one.

Do not look anything up. A question you can only answer with a search is a question you cannot answer in an interview.
L5 · rules first

Say the "score is not a verdict" line before the first question, not after. Students who think they are being judged stop answering honestly, and the diagnostic stops working.
